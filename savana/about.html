Our Story

From a small kitchen
to huge kitchen with the best experience

Savannah Bites was born in 2021 from the a streets resturant. What started as a small roadside eatery in Bwaise has grown into Kampala's most classic beloved dining place.

Today, we serve over 500 guests daily, offering discounts to our daily customers, Delicious meals, and continental favourites — all prepared with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and a whole lot of love.
